The research behind the signal

Open research, bounded claims

Read the work. Challenge the conclusions.

These are published editorial investigations into the ideas and experiments behind GhostMesh Scout. Each article identifies its evidence, strongest objections, and unresolved questions. A reviewed source is not an installed product; a synthetic experiment is not a production outcome; a research lead is not a verified person.

Our public commitment: preserve negative results, expose uncertainty and update judgments when the evidence changes. Private account information, raw research packets and security credentials stay outside the public site.
