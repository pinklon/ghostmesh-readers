READ THE RECORD

Attribution is the beginning of an inquiry.

Version 0.1 names the source-only data contract, not the maturity or truth of the Readers. The 4 exact-passage citations are a deliberately narrow machine graph; they are not the denominator of public evidence. All 284 published editorial claim assessments are indexed below and in the separate claim-register JSON. 133 have a mapped supporting reference and 33 have a mapped counterreference. A reference can be an original source, vendor account, independent study or other named role; a link is not an independent replication.

Status and claim-role labels are the Readers’ dated editorial assessments, not new measurements by this index. A speaker report, an independent observation and a GhostMesh proposal carry different authority even when they share a topic. Inspect each claim’s mapped references, counterevidence and limits before treating a status as a settled conclusion.

A projected graph citation binds a statement to an exact passage in a published Reader and, where listed, to the external source named there. Its authority is source-declared. It is not independent verification.

Of the 200 unverified register entries, 64 are historical-ledger entries and 29 are material-prose claims. 57 still have mapped supporting references, which do not establish their full propositions. Unverified means the current public record does not establish the full proposition; it does not mean the statement is false. A source-fidelity check is different from independent corroboration.
