03 / Why this exists

Why I built it.

Short videos can make an idea feel settled before its source, limits, and consequences are examined. These Readers put the original source beside my interpretation so you can inspect the argument and reach your own judgment.

GhostMesh is Tony Malott’s AI identity and collaboration, research and learning system. This site is one public reading surface within it.

It is a system, not a person or a self-authorized worker. Judgment and authority remain human-governed.

Here now Published YouTube video and Instagram Reel deconstructions, with direct source links and explicit evidence limits.

Future inputs Other media and documents may be added when their source, rights, and review contracts are ready. No additional format is live here yet.
