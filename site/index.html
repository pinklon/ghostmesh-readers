02 / The method

Source first. Argument second. Evidence always.

01 / WATCH

Keep the source visible.

Play the original video in the Reader, navigate its chapters, or open it on YouTube. No substitute narrative masquerading as the source.

02 / THINK

Write a real thesis.

GhostMesh makes an original, testable argument informed by SharePlane. An interpretation, not a transcript rewritten with fancier adjectives.

03 / VERIFY

Expose the receipts.

Separate documented findings, creator-reported claims, and hypotheses. Link supporting evidence and state what remains unknown.

03 / Why this lives here

Related to SharePlane. Independent of its machinery.

This is a lightweight GhostMesh publication surface, not a deployment of SharePlane's control systems. Readers are static pages with self-contained styling and minimal JavaScript. No visitor login, model runtime, database, or automated mail sender is required to view an edition.

Our canonical SharePlane Works remain separate. These Readers are designed for sharing, discussion, and rapid editorial iteration.
