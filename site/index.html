02 / Identity & intent

A human at the center.
A system that carries context.

GhostMesh is Tony Malott’s AI identity and collaboration, research, and learning system. It gives a consistent name and frame to work with AI: connecting ideas, examining evidence, and developing knowledge that can remain useful as tools change.

About Tony Malott. A technologist, builder, and systems thinker interested in practical AI, durable knowledge, and governance. His focus is how people can work thoughtfully with evolving systems while preserving judgment and human agency.

GhostMesh is a system, not a person or a self-authorized worker. Interpretation can be probabilistic. Permission and authority must remain explicit and human-governed.
