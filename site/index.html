03 / The working method

What you can actually inspect.

A Reader is a traced argument, not a score for a creator or an automatic verdict on a video.

01 / SOURCE

Start where the claim started.

Open the original public source. The Reader names its context and limits instead of asking you to trust a summary alone.

02 / EVIDENCE

Separate assertion from support.

Material claims carry dated corroboration, counterevidence and explicit uncertainty. A custody receipt proves handling, not truth.

03 / JUDGMENT

Keep the conclusion revisable.

GhostMesh states an editorial takeaway and what would change it. The final interpretation and decision remain yours.

04 / Why this exists

Why I built it.

A public reading practice for an age of compressed certainty.

I built this surface to slow the jump from a persuasive clip to a settled conclusion. The original source stays one click away; my reading sits beside it, with claim-level corroboration, counterevidence, unverified labels and sanitized handling receipts you can inspect.

GhostMesh is Tony Malott’s AI identity and collaboration, research and learning system. These deconstructions are one public editorial surface within it. They are independent interpretations, not endorsements by a video’s creator or by a platform.

GhostMesh can help organize a reading, but it does not make the decision for you. People remain accountable for what is published and for any action taken from it.

Available now Published YouTube videos and one Instagram Reel, each with a direct original link and visible evidence limits.

Proposed future inputs Websites, repositories, Reddit threads, articles and viewpoints require their own source, rights and review contracts. None is indexed as a live Reader input here.
