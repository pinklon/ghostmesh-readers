### Daily run disposition and release proof

Collection observations alone are not publication candidates. This is a handoff record, not a new queue or an automatic semantic verdict.
- Before any public-copy handoff, name the source version and original date, check the latest revision or successor, record why older evidence remains relevant, and separate source-reported behavior from an executed observation or account entitlement. Apply the six-month disclosure below when it is triggered. The independent reviewer must accept the exact source-bound copy; tests cannot establish the truth of a capability claim.
- Classify the work before release. The former `routine-source-report-v1` standing publication authority is suspended for new works. A source-only observation is useful research input, but cannot become a public GhostMesh article by passing provenance, privacy and layout checks alone. Essays, interpretations, recommendations, uncertain capability claims and new relationships retain the ordinary owner decisions. A later revision keeps the same canonical record and adds a material-update event only when the actual public revision is released; it does not create a second work.
- The register and release ledger project the Research Library, Scout landing, relationship JSON, sitemap and feed. The older `/scout/essays/` page is a curated static index; if it carries a revised work, its card title, summary and source scope must match the canonical record on the exact candidate head. - After the qualified unchanged-head merge, read back the actual Netlify production deploy and live canonical article, Library card, relevant curated index card, approved links or graph edges, sitemap, unique feed event and public/private boundary. A passed source test, preview or merge alone is not a live publication receipt.

## Source freshness at selection

- Record the source's original publication or applicable version date separately from the day Scout found, inspected or published it. Before selecting a finding, inspect the primary source's latest revision and any successor or superseding document. Identify which version supports each material claim; a current retrieval date does not make old text a new development.
- If the relevant source is more than six calendar months old at review, it may still be selected. The public page and card must visibly say **Older but relevant**, give the original source/version date, explain the current question that makes it worth revisiting, and link or describe the latest revision/supersession check and any material difference. Do not present resurfacing as a new announcement. If the source date or current applicability cannot be established, keep that uncertainty explicit and hold routine publication for ordinary editorial review.
- Preserve the distinction between a newly announced change and older evidence newly relevant to a current question. Do not use an age cutoff to discard a useful source, and do not infer that an older version's rules still govern a newer one. The independent reviewer checks the dated source, current applicability, public label and reason for resurfacing before accepting a release.

## New source reports: standing publication suspended October 29, 2026

The October 26 standing policy allowed a short, accurately attributed source observation to publish after independent source review and mechanical release checks. That admitted pages without an original GhostMesh argument. Tony corrected that publication standard on October 29 after two thin reports reached production. **Do not publish another new `routine-source-report-v1` work under standing authority.** The hosted `check_scout_routine_release.py` gate fails closed on a new record in this class. Existing published reports retain their identities, dates and feed history; this change does not silently unpublish or rewrite them.

Keep source-only findings in the private research handoff. A public candidate needs a distinct governing claim, the GhostMesh judgment and its consequence, source evidence that supports and bounds that judgment, a serious objection, and a useful implication or test. Its independent reviewer must assess whether the argument adds substance beyond paraphrasing the source and whether the strongest objection is treated fairly. Length, headings, a checklist of citations or a positive source-fidelity receipt cannot substitute for that judgment. Prepare such a candidate through the ordinary editorial and exact-head release path, including Tony's semantic and visual acceptance; do not relabel a thin report as an essay to bypass this gate. No new standing analytical class is implied by this correction.

For a correction to an already published thin report, preserve its canonical URL, record ID and original first-release event. Bind any substantive amendment to exact reviewed copy and add a material-update event only when that amendment actually publishes. Use the existing PR, CI, preview, owner editorial and exact-head release gates, then read back the live page, Library, graph, feed and sitemap. The corrected source-only note may remain live while this separate amendment is reviewed; do not erase release history.

This suspension creates no new collector, inference service, account access or publication queue. Roll back a harmful release through the existing repository revert and Netlify deploy path, preserving its receipt and publishing a correction when subscribers may have received the item; do not silently rewrite history.
