# Reader claim and evidence posture audit

Source: `content/catalog.json` at the PR #25 draft source checkpoint, 2026-10-19. This audit classifies the existing public register. It does not change a claim status, certify a private capture, or independently verify a third-party assertion.

| Recorded status | Claim records | What the label permits |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Unverified | 131 | The current public record does not establish the full proposition to the editorial standard used by that Reader. It does not say the proposition is false. |
| Partially corroborated | 14 | A named source supports a bounded part of the proposition; the remaining scope and limits still matter. |
| Corroborated | 2 | The cited public material supports the specific recorded proposition in its stated scope. It does not verify a whole video, vendor performance, or a current deployment. |

The 148 records are **claim-register entries**, not 148 independent factual discoveries. Among the 131 labeled unverified, 64 are `historical-ledger-entry` and 33 are `material-prose-claim`. Those 97 entries have no catalog references. The remaining 34 unverified entries span source attribution, speaker assertions, project and first-person descriptions, editorial inference, and five `external-evidence` entries. The five unverified external-evidence entries include counterevidence links; an unverified status is not synonymous with an empty link list.

Across all 148 records, 16 have nonempty `references`, six have nonempty `counterevidence`, and 22 have either kind of link. A link is not an independent replication by itself. Some vendor primary documentation can substantiate what a vendor documents without proving measured product performance. Publication dates, last-file-update dates, and measurement dates are different facts.

## Four distinct assertions

1. **Source attribution** identifies where a statement appears in the original public source. A timestamp, quotation, or faithful paraphrase can be correct while the underlying claim remains unverified.
2. **Corroboration** asks whether a separate qualified public record supports the proposition within a stated time and scope. Partial support cannot be promoted to whole-claim truth.
3. **Editorial judgment** is GhostMesh's analysis, verdict, or proposed architecture implication. It is a reasoned interpretation that remains open to correction, not a source fact or authority to act.
4. **Unsupported or unresolved** means the available public record is insufficient for the full assertion. It should stay visibly unverified. Counterevidence can be present without making the proposition settled.

The source-only Knowledge v0.1 projection contains 13 Reader identities and one admitted Inbox claim/citation tied to an exact published passage and external source link. It is a narrower contract than the 148-entry Reader claim register. Its citation proves traceability of the statement to a source link, not factual truth or a cross-site SharePlane relationship.

This audit did not open or publish private transcripts, captions, media, or source packets. A future status change needs claim-specific source review, dates, qualification, and an editorial decision. Retain the original status until that work is recorded.
