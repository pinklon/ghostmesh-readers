## GhostMesh thesis and selective route, proposed

**Thesis:** The strongest version of this interview is not that every safety mechanism is fake. Robinson says there are real brakes. His challenge is whether those brakes, test conditions and release decisions remain legible and binding as the system changes faster. OpenAI's published framework and dated pause reports counter a categorical no-controls claim; the July agent intrusion and acknowledged escalation gap show why a process document alone is not proof of operational control. A credible public safety case should connect a warning to a threshold, test environment, accountable decision and independent recheck. This is GhostMesh's inference from the bounded record, not a finding that a civilizational outcome is certain.
