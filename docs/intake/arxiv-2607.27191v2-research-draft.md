## Candidate thesis and reader decision, pending owner lock

The study tests whether agents can do more than complete the engineering steps of research. In two selected shadow evaluations, the original paper authors rejected the agent-produced papers while reporting substantial autonomous engineering work. That is an informative case study about research judgment under this setup. It is not a universal ceiling on AI research ability, a head-to-head matched human comparison, or proof that all future scaffolds fail. A Reader could help researchers and AI deployment leads ask separately whether an agent ran experiments and whether the experiments meaningfully answered the question. No architecture change follows from this source alone.

Working recommendation: **Worth studying** for people evaluating autonomous research workflows; skip as evidence for a universal AGI or automation timetable. Editorial category/verdict and final wording require Tony's review. This working note has no public Reader identity, permalink, relations, or admitted knowledge claim.
