## Standard instructions

- Score every card with the same effort budget: up to five minutes and one additional public-source open, if a source is discoverable.
- Relevance: `0` none; `1` speculative; `2` concrete possibility; `3` strong source-backed connection; `ABSTAIN` when insufficient evidence.
- Novelty: `0` familiar; `1` variation; `2` materially different; or `ABSTAIN`.
- Surprise: `0` expected; `1` somewhat unexpected; `2` highly unexpected; or `ABSTAIN`.
- For each ID: record relevance, novelty, surprise, strongest objection, source-review sufficiency, and a written rationale. Do not infer identity from matching names.
- Return a complete independent ratings JSON with schema `scout-independent-review-labels-v0`, `ratings` array, and one item for each ID. Each must have `review_id`, `ghostmesh_relevance`, and a reasoned `rationale` (at least twelve characters). Include the extra rubric fields too.

**Important:** This packet preserves titles and brief source excerpts, not verified source-body excerpts. Blindness is about known cohort and prior verdicts, not perfect concealment of origin. Do not consult the sealed key or previous Scout comparison report until judgments are finalized.
